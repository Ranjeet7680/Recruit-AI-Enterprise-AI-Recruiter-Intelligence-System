        arrow_right How does AI Candidate Match scoring work?

        The scoring engine maps candidate profiles against the job description using vector search embeddings (KNN similarity) and extracts feature matching scores across key competencies. You can adjust weights in the Job description parameters.

        arrow_right What is the demographic bias shield?

        When Demographic Masking is toggled ON, the candidates list masks details like gender, ethnicity, and age to promote fair, merit-based hiring evaluations.

        arrow_right How do I change my workspace's brand accent colors?

        Administrators can configure the system-wide styling token in the Organization section by editing the Branding Hex Accent color code.

        arrow_right How can I redeem referral bonuses?

        Sharing your invite code rewards your team with free billing credits (up to $50 per team sign-up) applied automatically at the start of your billing cycle.

        Privacy & Data Governance Policy

        1. Vector Embedding Processing

        Candidate resumes parsed by TalentMind AI are processed locally into anonymized vector embeddings. These embeddings represent numerical feature representations used for similarity computations and are completely decoupled from personally identifiable markers.

        2. GDPR & Compliance Logs

        All candidates data transactions, audit operations, and AI scores evaluations are tracked within the Compliance log registry. Clients retain full control over data retention parameters and can issue hard deletion requests directly from their workspace settings console.

        3. Demographic Shield Mechanism

        Our Bias Shield guarantees that demographic fields (gender, age, ethnicity, location coordinates) are removed prior to AI candidate matching queries execution, protecting enterprise recruiting workflows from systematic bias hazards.

        4. Data Safety Updates

        Our data safety models are checked daily against international regulations. Updates to our security policies are posted in the Settings console under app releases.
